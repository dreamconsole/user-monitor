User Monitor System

The User Monitor System is a sophisticated, all-in-one platform designed to help modern organizations understand how their teams work. Whether your team is remote, hybrid, or in-office, this system provides the transparency and insights needed to boost focus, manage workloads fairly, and ensure high-level productivity across the board.

At its core, the system is a digital workspace assistant. It helps managers see "the big picture" of their team's activity while giving employees tools to manage their own schedules, breaks, and output.

Unlike old-school tracking, this platform is designed for transparency. Employees can see exactly what data is being shared, and managers use this data to identify burnout, reward high performers, or help those who might be struggling with time management.

Access Levels & Three Logins

The system is organized into three distinct "levels," each with its own specialized login and dashboard experience to ensure users only see what they need.

Level A SuperAdmin Login (The Platform Owner)

Designed for high-level system administrators who manage the entire platform. This level provides a master control panel to oversee all organizations, manage subscriptions, and monitor global system health.

Level B Organization Admin & Manager Login (The Leadership)

Designed for business owners, HR managers, and Team Leads. It provides full visibility into their specific company or team, allowing them to set rules, view timelines, and generate reports.

Level C Employee & User Login (The Team Member)

Designed for every staff member being tracked. A personal dashboard focused on their own productivity, stats, and profile management, connecting seamlessly with the Desktop Agent.

The Desktop Agent (.EXE)

The Desktop Agent is the heart of the monitoring system. It's a lightweight application (often installed as an .exe on Windows) that runs quietly on your computer.

  • Automatic Tracking: Detects when you're active or idle without you having to click anything.
  • Local Intelligence: Even if your internet goes down, the agent keeps tracking your work and syncs everything once you're back online.
  • Low System Impact: Designed to be extremely light, so it won't slow down your computer or apps.
  • Visibility: A simple interface shows you if the agent is currently "In Work" or "On Break."

Automated Screen Captures

The system uses randomized periodic screenshots to provide a visual log of your workday accomplishments.

  • Frequency Control: Organization admins decide the interval (e.g., a snapshot every 5 or 10 minutes).
  • Privacy Awareness: Screenshots are only taken when you are officially in "In Work" mode.
  • Visual Proof: Helps managers see the complexity of the tasks you're handling and provides proof of work for billing or performance reviews.

Customization & Control Center

Every organization works differently, so the system provides deep customization options.

Organization Custom Settings

Admins can define organization-wide rules on the Settings Page:

  • Screenshot Policy: Turn screenshots on or off for the entire company.
  • Idle Thresholds: Set how many minutes of inactivity should trigger an "Idle" status.
  • Feature Toggles: Enable or disable specific features like AFK tracking or specialized break types.

User-Specific Overrides

We recognize that "one size doesn't fit all." Managers can customize features for individual users:

  • High-Trust Roles: Disable screenshots for senior management while keeping them active for others.
  • Specific Breaks: Allow custom break types or durations for specific employees based on needs.
  • Custom Intervals: Adjust how often data is captured for specific high-stakes projects.

SuperAdmin Master Panel

For the highest level of oversight, the SuperAdmin Panel is a dedicated area for platform owners.

  • Organization Management: Create, suspend, or upgrade entire company accounts.
  • Platform Health: Monitor system performance and storage usage globally.
  • Database Optimization: Keep the system fast by managing and cleaning up old logs and large physical files.

Detailed Features

1. Smart Productivity Tracking

The system automatically learns how you work, categorizing time into Active Work, Idle Time, and AFK (Away From Keyboard) status.

2. Application & Website Insights

The platform tracks what software and websites are being used most frequently, allowing you to categorize them as "Productive," "Neutral," or "Non-Productive."

3. Advanced Break Management & Groups

Ensures employees are taking healthy breaks throughout the day while keeping policies organized.

  • Break Groups: Group multiple break types together (e.g., "Full-Time Shift Group").
  • Assign Group: Assign specific break policies to teams or individuals with one click.
  • Policy Logic: Automatic calculation of Paid vs. Unpaid components based on assigned group rules.

Technology Overview

Interface (Dashboard) Built with React, a leading technology for fast, responsive web applications.
Brain (Backend) Powered by Node.js, designed to handle massive amounts of real-time activity data.
Storage (Database) Uses PostgreSQL, a secure and reliable database for protecting sensitive data.
Desktop App Built with Electron, allowing seamless operation on both Windows and Mac.
